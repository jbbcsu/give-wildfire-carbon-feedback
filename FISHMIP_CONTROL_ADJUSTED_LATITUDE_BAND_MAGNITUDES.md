# FishMIP control-adjusted latitude-band magnitudes

This outcome-blind extension partitions each late-century (2081--2090)
control-adjusted trajectory into the same five fixed latitude bands used by
the sign audits. Each trajectory remains normalized to its own forcing/model
historical global mean, so absolute BOATS and EcoOcean levels are never
averaged. Band contributions reconcile exactly to each trajectory's global
normalized adjustment.

Under SSP1-2.6, all four trajectory means are negative in the two Southern
Hemisphere bands. Three of four are negative in the tropics, northern
midlatitudes, and northern high latitudes. Band means span -12.56 to -0.34
percentage points in the southern high-latitude band and -8.20 to +13.68
points in northern midlatitudes.

Under SSP5-8.5, all four means are negative in the southern midlatitudes,
tropics, and northern midlatitudes. Those ranges are -13.42 to -7.07,
-15.02 to -7.27, and -28.57 to -5.35 percentage points, respectively. Three
of four means are negative in each high-latitude band. The IPSL/EcoOcean
northern-high trajectory is the exception at +1.57 points even though 71% of
its weighted cells are nonnegative; the band mean and cell-sign share answer
different questions and are both retained.

The result sharpens the spatial structural sensitivity but does not identify
a causal forced response. Historical and future social forcing differ at the
2015 join; latitude bands are neither countries nor EEZs; and no observed
catch, matched CO2 pulse, probability, allocation, welfare, damage, or SCC
gate is cleared. The machine-readable receipt is
`data/provenance/fishmip_control_adjusted_latitude_band_magnitudes_20260831.json`.
