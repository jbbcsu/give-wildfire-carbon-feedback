# Normalized market sensitivity with published elasticity candidates

Registered before calculation. Source parameter transcription is confined to
the six columns of published Appendix Table A8, not a selected favorable
column. Its FAS alternative is not the paper's main FAO baseline. Text was
read from the publisher; page screenshots returned a cache miss. Do not claim
visual verification or an executed replication. Retain this limitation in the
source configuration. No source code or raw replication data are reused.

Cross-check each supply/demand pair against its printed reciprocal-gap price
multiplier. Rounded coefficients cannot reproduce every printed multiplier
exactly. Calculate the interval implied by ±0.0005 for each three-decimal
coefficient, and require overlap with the printed multiplier's ±0.005 interval.
Do not adjust transcribed point estimates to force equality.

Pass all six point-estimate pairs to the independent single closed-market
core. Use demand magnitude = minus signed demand elasticity; require downward
demand and upward supply. The published SEs are retained but **not** sampled:
joint covariance is missing, and unconstrained marginal normal draws can leave
the model's domain. The six models are a specification sensitivity, not six
independent draws or a confidence interval. No empirical prior is selected.

Use explicitly hypothetical productivity factors0.99,1,1.01 and both declared
yield-to-supply conventions (horizontal output and fixed input cost). Normalize
baseline market value to1; all surplus results are fractions of that value,
not dollars. No observed or projected yields, prices, emissions or GDP enter.
Calculate price/quantity ratios, consumer/producer/total surplus changes and
the analytic local derivative with respect to log productivity. Check market
clearing, surplus accounting and the derivative against centered finite
differences. Report all36 states. Do not compare hypothetical 1% productivity
shocks with the historical U.S. rainfall contrasts as if they were forecasts.

This is a transparent economic-accounting sensitivity only. It does not
replicate the source's ethanol-policy experiment, identify climate-driven
supply shifts, justify crop/country transfer, model market integration or
long-run adaptation, supply baseline market values, or value missing sectors.

monitored job, numerical threads one; no download, rehydration or installation.
