# Published market-elasticity route: source checkpoint

The AER publisher confirms Roberts and Schlenker (2013), *Identifying Supply
and Demand Elasticities of Agricultural Commodities: Implications for the US
Ethanol Mandate*, 103(6):2265–2295. Its abstract describes identification using
past yield shocks, inventories and futures prices, and an application to corn,
rice, soybeans and wheat. The publisher supplies separate replication-package
and supplemental-appendix links. [Publisher article and materials](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.103.6.2265).

not a verified calibration. Exact published elasticities, equations,
