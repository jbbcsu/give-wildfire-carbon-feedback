# Armed-conflict integration contract

A future global module must provide, for identical GIVE baseline and marginal
pulse paths, annual country-level changes in conflict onset, incidence, severity,
and duration with uncertainty. Climate exposure must be mapped through a stated
causal estimand whose support covers the projection domain; pooled standardized
meta-analysis effects alone are insufficient.

Valuation must itemize mortality, morbidity, property/capital loss, output and
labor loss, displacement, public expenditure, education/human capital, and
nonpecuniary welfare. Each item must be compared with enabled GIVE modules.
Transfers, reconstruction spending, and military/fiscal costs require a global
welfare treatment rather than automatic classification as damage. Persistent GDP
effects must replace overlapping sector damages or enter a mutually exclusive
macro sensitivity, never be added wholesale.

Required validation includes historical hindcasts, alternative conflict datasets
and definitions, leave-region-out performance, placebo climate timing, sensitivity
to institutions and income, and propagation of climate, causal, baseline-risk,
severity, duration, and valuation uncertainty. Until these conditions are met,
the numeric gate remains closed.
