# Exploratory geographic influence check

Before execution: omit each observed state in turn from the existing
non-irrigated corn and soybean samples, for the baseline and added stage
maximum-temperature specifications. Use the previously selected quantity
form for corn and quantity-plus-timing for soybeans. Retain every omission,
including fit failures and sign reversals; do not choose a new model here.

Keep county and state-year fixed effects and original sample gates. Evaluate
the +100 mm contrast at the FULL sample's median rainfall, unchanged across
omissions, so shifts reflect fitted response rather than reference rainfall.
For soybeans also report the stage-3-to-stage-2 ten-percentage-point contrast.
These are coefficient influence diagnostics, not held-out predictions,
independent validation, a spatial covariance estimator, or causal claims.
Previously observed results inform this exploratory choice of analysis.

Only saved inputs are used. One monitored job, 1 GiB sampled RSS budget;
compact output, no downloads. New daily heat exposure remains a separate task.
