# FishMIP control-adjusted latitude scenario separation

This audit compares SSP5-8.5 with SSP1-2.6 inside each matched climate
forcing, ecosystem model, fixed decade, and latitude band of the frozen
20-file structural-sensitivity matrix. It subtracts the already normalized
control-adjusted band means; it does not average absolute ecosystem-model
levels or assign model probabilities.

Across the exact 60 matched trajectory cells, SSP5-8.5 is more negative than
SSP1-2.6 in 43. The near-term pattern is mixed: none of the four southern
high-latitude trajectories is more negative under SSP5-8.5, while three of
four northern high-latitude trajectories are. By 2041--2050, all four
structures show more-negative SSP5-8.5 changes in the southern midlatitudes,
tropics, and northern midlatitudes. By 2081--2090, unanimity extends to the
northern high latitudes, while the southern high latitudes remain split two
and two.

This qualifies a global scenario narrative. Stronger forcing produces broad
central- and northern-band separation later in the century, but not a uniform
ordering across all locations, models, or periods. The audit is not a causal
forced response because the control joins change social forcing at 2015. It
uses total-catch density rather than observed catch, performs no country or
EEZ allocation, contains no matched CO2 pulse or welfare translation, and
does not authorize damages or an SCC input.
