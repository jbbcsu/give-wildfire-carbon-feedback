This appendix is a transparency scaffold for the human author's AI-assisted research-programming workflow. It should be completed before submission with the original prompt and selected follow-up prompts copied from the research log.

## Original Research Prompt

The original prompt asked the assistant to audit the Rennert et al. (2022) GIVE replication archive, determine whether wildfire, biomass-burning or non-anthropogenic CO2 emissions were already represented, design a transparent wildfire-CO2 extension if not, run baseline and wildfire SCC experiments, diagnose double counting and produce a technical memo, figures, reproducible scripts and a manuscript.

## Selected Follow-Up Prompt Themes

- RFF-SP aggregate CO2 pathways and whether wildfire-related carbon is already embedded.
- Double-counting risk from AFOLU, LULUCF, natural-stock changes and gross fire emissions.
- Climate-driven biomass-burning projections, including boreal Canada and Russia.
- Use of residual net-persistent fire CO2 rather than gross fire emissions as the central case.
- Deterministic stress tests, including Canada-2023-scale and high forest-fire projection scenarios.
- Sectoral marginal-damage diagnostics with saved marginal damages and sectoral values.
- Fire-flow versus atmospheric-stock percentage interpretation.
- Map revisions distinguishing source attribution from damage incidence.
- Nature-family manuscript style and movement of code-level audit details to the supplement.
- Transparent but limited disclosure of AI-assisted research programming.
