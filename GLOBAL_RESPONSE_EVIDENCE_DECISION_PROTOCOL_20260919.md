# Frozen global agricultural-response evidence decision

This synthesis is registered after all contributing results were inspected. It
does not create a new holdout and must not be described as prospective model
selection. Its purpose is to prevent a physically interesting climate signal
from being promoted to a yield, damage, or SCC relationship without predictive
evidence.

Bind and read only three independently audited aggregate results: the global
country-held-out yield comparison, the same-cell historical/future weather
support diagnostic, and the GMST-normalized terminal weather benchmark. Do not
read coefficients, row outcomes, or raw climate.

A candidate empirical family is ineligible for global production if the
country-held-out result says production promotion is unauthorized, lacks new
independent outcomes, or lacks a new untouched holdout. Even absent that global
gate, require both maize and soybean to have all five valid country folds and
a supported paired country bootstrap. Quantity must beat both heat controls
and zero change on pooled and equal-country RMSE with upper 95% bootstrap
bounds below zero. Distribution and each drought family must beat quantity by
the same rule. A point estimate, in-sample association, or climate-feature sign
cannot substitute for this test.

Separately record whether all six GMST-normalized ESM/scenario endpoints agree
on the sign of wet days, maximum dry spell, Rx1day and Rx5day. This physical
signal determines whether distributional features remain reportable climate
sensitivities, not whether they enter a damage function. Record future
same-cell min/max exceedance ranges as a transport warning; never clip values.

If no family passes, retain seasonal precipitation quantity with temperature
controls only as the parsimonious *research benchmark*, retain distribution and
drought as separately reported competitors, and set empirical response,
damage, and SCC promotion to false. Published crop-model rainfall responses
remain structural benchmarks rather than replacements for failed empirical
gates. No selection may depend on damage or SCC magnitude.
