# Global historical moisture associations (exploratory)

Registered before this new fit. This is a separate descriptive analysis, not
an export of the predictive evaluator's coefficients or a production response.
The existing causal registry and predictive promotion rules remain unchanged.

Use the hash-verified direct/heat/scPDSI common-support maize and soybean
tables, with the existing fixed-MIRCA basis-before-weighting construction.
Use consecutive log-yield differences ending in 1983–2010 only, retaining
the 2012–2016 terminal test untouched. Equal grid-pair weights are not
production weights. These are aggregate-regime outcomes, not rainfed yields.

Fit four moisture families separately: quantity; quantity plus distribution;
seasonal scPDSI; and three-stage scPDSI. Every model includes three stage mean
temperature and three daily-Tmax degree-day differences (29 C maize, 30 C soy),
as in the completed predictive benchmark. Compare its quadratic end-year
controls against unrestricted end-year intercepts. First differencing removes
time-invariant cell levels; year intercepts allow common annual shocks. These
controls do not establish identification of a causal weather response.

Preserve all 16 fits. Report partial fitted log-yield contrasts and transformed
percentage contrasts: +0.1 in the weighted log(1+seasonal precipitation/mm)
index; a +0.1 stage-2 share shift offset by stage 3 (stage 1 unchanged); and
+1 scPDSI unit for seasonal mean or, in the stage model, all three stages.
The rain index is NOT log of area-weighted rainfall. Its +0.1 shift corresponds
to multiplying (1+rainfall/mm) by exp(0.1) in both calendar regimes before
fixed-share averaging. It is not exactly a 10% rain increase and is not a
projected climate change. Timing is a formal partial contrast, not proof that
other included distribution metrics can physically remain fixed in daily rain.

Use normal-approximation CR1 cluster-score intervals with fixed 10-degree and
20-degree latitude/longitude blocks (all years of a block clustered together).
Report block counts, design rank/condition, sample counts, all failures, and
the year-control sensitivity. Blocks need not be independent: GDHY may share
administrative source data across boundaries, and teleconnections remain.
Intervals are conditional descriptive sensitivity, not causal uncertainty or
correction for model search. Do not choose specifications by significance.

Stream 10-degree latitude bands; retain only block cross-products. Verify the
moment-based fit and cluster covariance against direct synthetic calculations
before real fits. Hash code, this protocol, input data and assembly receipts.
Use one monitored existing-data job at a time; no acquisition or large output.
No fitted response bundle, future damage, welfare, or SCC output is produced.
