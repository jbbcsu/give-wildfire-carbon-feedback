# Published historical counterfactual route: ATTRICI

Literature-first assessment; no counterfactual climate data acquired or crop effects
estimated in this assessment. Main-text methods and discussion were read;
the supplement and implementation code were not audited.

## Published evidence

[Mengel et al. (2021), ATTRICI v1.1](https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/14/5269/2021/index.html)
provides daily 0.5° historical counterfactual GSWP3-W5E5 climate. It removes
long-term changes associated with smoothed global temperature, irrespective
of their cause. Day-of-year harmonics permit changing seasonality. Precipitation
uses Bernoulli wet/dry occurrence and gamma wet-day intensity: occurrence and
mean vary with warming; gamma shape varies seasonally, not with warming.
The dry-day threshold is0.1mm/day. Quantile mapping retains relative ranks;
some dry-to-wet conversions are randomized. Physical consistency is not
guaranteed, and observational artifacts can invalidate counterfactuals.
The method is explicitly not a replacement for anthropogenic-forcing
experiments. Code is GPL and archived at DOI10.5281/zenodo.5032065;
published counterfactual data at DOI10.5281/zenodo.5036364.

## Project interpretation and proposed decision gates

This is a credible existing route to evaluate before fitting another custom
historical precipitation counterfactual. It is not an annual-total-only method.
Our inference: a changing marginal distribution is not, by itself, a validated
model of changes in storm persistence or all drought sequences. Recompute our
crop-calendar totals, stage shares, dry spells, Rx5day and concentration from
paired factual/counterfactual daily files; assess each, not just annual means.
Retain our1mm/day feature threshold and register0.1mm/day sensitivity separately.

First resolve the current public catalogue's exact factual/counterclim product,
version, license, construction period and source lineage. Do not assume the
2021 publication's artifact equals every current v1.3 file. Use small two-row
server subsets only. Check dates, nonnegativity, temperature ordering, seasonal
climatologies and joint heat/moisture support. Keep source artifacts and known
inhomogeneities explicit. Reusing published output avoids a new heavy training
job; it does not prove the counterfactual is valid in every crop region.

A subsequent response analysis would first need an explicitly approved
estimation/transport contract with temperature, CO2 and management controls.
Precipitation-only swaps require a pre-specified interaction allocation and
joint-support checks; do not add drought-index and precipitation damages.
Label any eventual result as conditional historical-trend attribution, not
anthropogenic emissions attribution or SCC. Future marginal-emissions climate
response, adaptation, welfare and GIVE-pulse validation remain separate gates.

Next: catalogue/lineage feasibility, then a small paired-climate diagnostic.
No claim of a new emulator, literature novelty, or completed yield damages.

## Additional published emulator lead (abstract-level review only)

[Heinze-Deml et al. (2021), LLAAE v1.0](https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/14/4977/2021/gmd-14-4977-2021.html)
models daily precipitation conditional on large-scale circulation using an
autoencoder with a linear latent adjustment. The reported demonstration is
European winter precipitation at12km using a50member regional-model ensemble,
not a global crop-season marginal-emissions emulator. This is evidence that
related published machinery exists, not proof of direct transferability here.
Read full methods, training requirements and code/license before adopting it.
No download, training or claim of comprehensive literature coverage is made.
