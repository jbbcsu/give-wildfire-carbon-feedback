# FishMIP structural-contrast sensitivity

This audit compares two distinct sources of structural dependence inside the
frozen 2-forcing by 2-ecosystem-model FishMIP matrix. Within each scenario,
decade, and latitude band, it calculates EcoOcean-minus-BOATS contrasts under
each climate forcing and IPSL-CM6A-LR-minus-GFDL-ESM4 contrasts under each
ecosystem model. It reports which pair has the larger root-mean-square contrast
without interpreting the four structures as probability draws.

The exact source is the checksum-bound latitude/window receipt. The executable
requires all 120 scenario/window/band/forcing/model rows, finite values, and the
source's closed forced-response, pulse, welfare, damage, and SCC gates.

This is a structural-sensitivity diagnostic. It is not a variance
decomposition, causal forced response, country or EEZ allocation, matched CO2
pulse, welfare result, damage estimate, or SCC input.
